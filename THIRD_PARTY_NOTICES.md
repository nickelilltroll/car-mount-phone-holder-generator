## Design Inspiration – Minimalist Holder

Model: **Brodit Holder for Samsung A6 and A70**  
Creator: **L00TW1CK**  
Source: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4774148  
License shown by Thingiverse: **Creative Commons – Attribution**

The Car Mount Phone Holder Generator's **Minimalist** option is an original
parametric implementation inspired by the overall layout of the referenced model:
a tapered central support, compact side grips, a central lower support, and a
vertical three-hole mounting concept.

The original mesh is not bundled with this repository. This notice is included to
provide attribution for the design inspiration.
