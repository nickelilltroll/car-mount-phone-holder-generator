## When a Commercial License Is Required

Please request a commercial license before doing any of the following:

- Selling physical phone holders generated with the software
- Selling STL, SCAD, mesh, or other generated model files
- Manufacturing holders for customers for payment
- Offering paid custom holder-generation services
- Using the generator as part of a business or commercial workflow
- Hosting the generator for customers, employees, clients, or subscribers
- Charging for access to the generator
- Integrating the generator into a commercial website, app, platform, or product
- Redistributing the generator as part of another product or service
- Using the generator or its source code to build a competing commercial service
- Creating a commercially distributed modified version
- Reselling, sublicensing, or white-labeling the generator
- Using project-specific code, presets, or design logic in another commercial product

If you are unsure whether your planned use is commercial, contact the project
owner before proceeding.

## Development and Modification Rights

The standard project license does not grant permission to modify or further
develop the source code.

A separate written agreement may grant rights such as:

- Internal modification
- Custom feature development
- Integration into another system
- White-label deployment
- Commercial hosting
- Redistribution
- Localization or translation
- Creation of additional holder designs
- Addition of proprietary phone/device databases
- OEM or reseller rights

The exact rights, limitations, term, territory, price, support, and update terms
will be agreed separately.

## Possible Commercial License Models

Depending on the intended use, licensing may be offered as one of the following:

### 1. Maker / Small Business License

For a person or small business that wants to sell a limited number of physical
holders generated using the software.

Possible terms may include:

- A fixed annual fee
- A per-unit royalty
- A sales-volume limit
- Attribution requirements
- No right to redistribute the generator itself

### 2. Professional Manufacturing License

For larger-scale production or commercial manufacturing of generated holders.

Possible terms may include:

- Annual or multi-year licensing
- Volume-based pricing
- Defined manufacturing rights
- Defined sales territories
- Optional support and updates

### 3. Developer / Integration License

For companies or developers who want to modify, integrate, host, or build upon
the generator.

Possible terms may include:

- Access to designated source code
- Permission to make agreed modifications
- Integration rights
- Deployment limits
- White-label rights where specifically agreed
- Maintenance and update terms

### 4. Custom / OEM Agreement

For device manufacturers, accessory companies, resellers, or other partners with
special requirements.

Terms are negotiated individually.

## What a Commercial License Does Not Automatically Include

Unless expressly stated in the signed agreement, a commercial license does not
automatically grant:

- Ownership of the underlying source code
- Rights to the Brodit trademark or any third-party trademark
- Patent rights
- Exclusivity
- The right to sublicense
- The right to publish the source code
- The right to remove third-party license notices
- Rights that belong to third-party libraries such as Three.js or JSCAD

Third-party components remain subject to their own licenses.

---

## Generated Models and Printed Products

Commercial permission may distinguish between:

1. selling physical printed holders;
2. selling or distributing generated digital model files; and
3. using or modifying the generator itself.

A license to sell physical printed holders does **not** automatically include the
right to redistribute STL/SCAD files or the generator source code.

This distinction should be stated explicitly in each commercial agreement.

## Requesting a License

When requesting commercial or development permission, include:

- Your name or company name
- Country
- Intended use
- Whether you want to sell physical products, digital files, software access, or
  a combination of these
- Expected sales or usage volume
- Whether source-code modification is required
- Whether hosting or integration is required
- Desired license duration
- Any exclusivity requirements

Contact the project owner through the official GitHub repository/profile or the
Buy Me a Coffee page above.

No commercial permission is granted until the copyright holder confirms the
permission in writing or both parties enter into a separate written agreement.
