## 10. Vehicle and Product Safety

A 3D-printed holder is a user-manufactured physical product.

Before use in a vehicle, you are responsible for verifying that:

- The holder is structurally sound
- Fasteners are secure
- The mount does not interfere with steering or vehicle controls
- The mount does not obstruct required visibility
- The holder does not interfere with airbags or other safety systems
- The selected material can withstand expected vehicle temperatures
- The phone remains secure during normal driving conditions
- Charging components are not placed under damaging mechanical stress

Do not use a holder that is cracked, deformed, loose, or otherwise unsafe.
